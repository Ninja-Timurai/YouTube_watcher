# You're Trying to Make Money the Wrong Way | The 70×7 Formula

_Channel: Kevin Trudeau · Published: 2026-10-07 · Length: 25:25 · Transcript: captions (en) · Summarised: 2026-10-09_  
_Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YV-ojSH_dHE_

## Bottom line

Kevin Trudeau's "70×7 formula": dream big, set a specific realistic income target, then for seven days write it 70 times a day in cursive, blue ink on white paper, saying it aloud, and release attachment. His claimed mechanism is brain "frequency" broadcast to the universe; no evidence offered.

## Key takeaways

- **Dream before targeting:** he says fantasising big ("dream expansion") must come first, or nothing else works. [02:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YV-ojSH_dHE&t=134s)
- **Specific, confident target:** pick an exact monthly income you are highly confident of reaching within a year. [05:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YV-ojSH_dHE&t=326s)
- **Never ask how:** thinking about how you will get it is, in his words, "a failure habit that will keep you broke". [07:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YV-ojSH_dHE&t=461s)
- **The ritual:** blue ink, white paper, cursive, spoken aloud, 70 times a day for 7 days. [19:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YV-ojSH_dHE&t=1186s)
- **Let go:** after 7 days throw the papers away and release attachment; he compares it to an Amazon order already placed. [21:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YV-ojSH_dHE&t=1288s)
